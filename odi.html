ODI Career Stats

Rohit Sharma is one of the greatest ODI batsmen of all time, known for his elegant stroke play and incredible ability to score big centuries 🎯. He is the only player in history to hit three double centuries in ODIs, including the world-record 264 🏆. With more than 11,000 runs, over 30 centuries, and a superb average around 49, Rohit has been a pillar of India’s batting lineup 🇮🇳. His record-breaking five centuries in the 2019 World Cup made him the top performer of the tournament 🌍🔥. Whether it's dominating fast bowlers or timing the ball effortlessly through the gaps, Rohit’s batting makes ODI cricket truly special ✨🏏.

Impact as an Opener

Rohit Sharma transformed into a run-machine after becoming a full-time ODI opener 🌟. His calm approach early in the innings and explosive hitting later make him one of the most feared openers worldwide 💥. With his effortless timing and ability to accelerate smoothly, he often sets the perfect platform for India’s big totals 🇮🇳.
