ROHIT SHARMA 💥

Rohit Sharma, lovingly called the "Hitman" 🔥, is one of the most gifted and elegant batsmen in world cricket 🏏. Born on April 30, 1987 🎂, he is known for his smooth timing, effortless six-hitting 💥, and calm presence on the field 😎. After making his debut in 2007, Rohit’s career changed completely when he began opening the innings in 2013, becoming one of the greatest ODI openers ever 🌟. He holds the world record for the highest ODI score—an unbelievable 264 🏆—and is the only player with three ODI double centuries 🎯. With more than 19,000 international runs and 50 centuries 🌍, he has earned his place among cricket’s modern legends. As a captain, he led India to the 2024 T20 World Cup victory🥇 and guided Mumbai Indians to multiple IPL titles ⭐. His blend of grace, power, and leadership makes him one of the most admired cricketers of his generation ❤️.
